How Do LLMs Predict the Next Word? (Simple Explanation)

DESCRIPTION (copy everything between the lines)

How can a chatbot write an essay by predicting just one word at a time? This video explains how large language models (LLMs) work: scoring every possible next word, picking one with a little randomness (temperature), repeating the loop, how the model learns by guessing hidden words, why it seems smart, and why it can still be wrong.
