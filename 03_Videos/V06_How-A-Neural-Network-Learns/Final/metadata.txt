How Does a Neural Network Learn? (Simple Explanation)

DESCRIPTION (copy everything between the lines)

Nobody tells an AI what a cat looks like, so how does it learn? This video explains how a neural network learns, step by step: layers and weights, making a guess, measuring the error, adjusting the weights with backpropagation, why small steps work (gradient descent), and how repeating it over many examples makes the error fall.
